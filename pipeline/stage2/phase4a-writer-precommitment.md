## Acceptance Criteria Paraphrase

D1 section_completeness (mandatory): all nine approved sections (I–IX) are written, plus four tables and two figures. No [MATERIAL GAP] remains unresolved at handoff to citation compliance.

D2 citation_density (mandatory): every factual statement about a design, number, or mechanism cites the paper it comes from. Absence claims ("no dynamic-graph PIM design", "no reliability evaluation") are written in bounded form (venues, years, search method) and cite the nearest prior work (Kim & Kim MEMSYS'20; GrAPL'25 TC).

D3 argument_blueprint_fidelity (mandatory): sections follow the approved outline's purpose column and the argument guards. Section VI carries the comparability and assumption-axis claims, each tied to extraction-sheet evidence.

D4 total_word_count (high): body text within ±10% of 7,000 words (6,300–7,700), excluding tables, captions and references.

D5 per_section_word_count (high): each section within ±15% of its allocation in outline.md.

D6 paragraph_structure (normal): each paragraph states a point, supports it with cited evidence, and links it to the section's question; transitions between sections are present.

D7 register_consistency (normal): formal computer-architecture register; no colloquialisms; the title wordplay stays in the title only.
