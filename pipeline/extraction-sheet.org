* C. Thesis status after full-text check
- *Assumption axis (mutability)*: strengthened. Every PIM and hybrid hardware design checked is static. The only PIM evidence with updates (triangle counting) is real-hardware, insert-only, non-incremental, and its advantage comes from the data format, not the architecture. DSA dynamic designs: JetStream → TDGraph → MEGA.
- *Real-PIM reality check*: strengthened. PrIM gives direct evidence that *BFS* (the RQ's flagship traversal) is bottlenecked by host-mediated inter-DPU synchronization.
- *Hybrid convergence*: Piccolo uses a DSA (GraphDynS) as its baseline and adds in-memory scatter-gather. FALA beats Piccolo and PEI. This is concrete evidence that the two camps are merging.
- *Security*: IMPACT evaluates on graph workloads, which strengthens the critical-systems section.
